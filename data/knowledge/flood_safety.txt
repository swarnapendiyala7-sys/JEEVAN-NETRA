FLOOD SAFETY GUIDELINES

Before a flood:
- Monitor official weather and emergency warnings.
- Keep emergency contact numbers available.
- Move important documents and valuables to a safe location.
- Avoid parking vehicles in low-lying areas.
- Prepare drinking water, food, medicines and a flashlight.

During a flood:
- Move to higher and safer ground when instructed.
- Do not walk or drive through moving flood water.
- Avoid contact with electrical equipment or fallen power lines.
- Follow instructions from local authorities.
- Keep children away from flooded roads and drainage channels.

After a flood:
- Do not enter damaged buildings until they are declared safe.
- Avoid flood water because it may contain sewage or dangerous debris.
- Report damaged roads, bridges, electrical systems and infrastructure.
- Drink safe or treated water.
